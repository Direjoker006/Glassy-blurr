# Glassy Blurr

A sleek, frosted glassmorphic theme for **Better Lyrics** designed for an immersive, distraction-free YouTube Music experience.

## ✨ Features
- **Floating Glassmorphic Dock:** A macOS-inspired translucent pill player bar with subtle hover elevation and rounded progress bar.
- **Depth-of-Field Lyric Focus:** Inactive lines softly blur out so your eyes stay locked onto the active karaoke lyrics—hovering over any line brings it sharply into focus.
- **Rich Ambient Mesh Background:** Deeply saturated, smooth blurred album artwork backdrop with a radial vignette.
- **Smooth Letter-Wave Karaoke:** Crisp word-by-word highlighting with subtle letter-wave animations and clean typography.
- **Frosted Pill Controls:** Rounded album art card paired with glassmorphic footer and autoscroll resume pills.
